Privacy Policy

for the Android app CallBaby Babyphone (de.beckstack.callbabyphone) · Last updated: 18 August 2026

In short
  • CallBaby has no server and no user account. We do not run a database about you.
  • The microphone signal never leaves your device. It is analysed on the device and discarded immediately — nothing is recorded, stored or transmitted.
  • Your phone number stays on the device. It is used only to place the call.
  • The only thing that leaves your device is what advertising requires — and you are asked about that on first launch.

2. What stays on your device

Microphone

The purpose of the app is to detect sounds in a child's room. To do so, CallBaby analyses the microphone signal while you have monitoring switched on. This analysis runs entirely on your device: the signal is filtered in short segments, its sound level measured and — if you chose the "crying" trigger — rated by a model that also resides on the device. The segment is then discarded.

No recording is created. No audio file is written, nothing is buffered, and nothing is sent to us or to third parties. At no point do we have access to what your microphone hears.

While monitoring is running, Android displays a persistent notification, so you can always see that the app is listening and stop it with a single tap.

Phone number and contact name

You enter the number to be called in an alarm, or pick it from your address book. It is stored, together with the country code and — when picked from the address book — the displayed name, in your device's local app settings. This information does not leave the device.

CallBaby does not request permission to access your contacts. Picking a contact uses Android's own contact picker; the app receives only the single entry you tap and never sees your address book.

Settings

Sensitivity, trigger type, delays, language and the remaining settings live in the app's local settings store and are removed when you uninstall the app.

The legal basis is your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR where such consent is required (see section 4). For non-personalised ads we rely on our legitimate interest in funding an app offered free of charge, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Apart from advertising, the app makes no internet connection. Monitoring itself works without a network.

4. Your consent and how to withdraw it

On first launch CallBaby shows a consent form provided by Google (User Messaging Platform), where you decide whether personalised advertising may be served. Your decision is stored on the device.

Withdrawal: you can reverse your decision at any time by clearing the app's data under Settings → Apps → CallBaby Babyphone → Storage; the form will appear again on the next launch. Independently of that, the advertising ID can be reset or deleted under Settings → Privacy → Ads, after which the device can no longer be recognised by the previous identifier.

Withdrawal takes effect for the future and does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out beforehand.

6. No analytics on our side

We use no analytics, tracking or crash reporting services. We do not learn how often you open the app, how it is configured, or whether it triggered.

Independently of this, Google Play collects its own data when an app is downloaded and used — such as install counts and, if you allowed it in your device settings, crash reports. Google is the controller for that; we see only aggregated, non-personal reports.

7. Retention

The information stored on your device (phone number, settings, consent decision) remains until you change it, clear the app data or uninstall the app. Nothing is stored on our side because nothing reaches us. Retention of advertising data follows Google's terms.

8. Your rights

You have the rights to access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21 GDPR).

In practice: because we receive no data about you, we cannot provide information about you or delete anything — there is nothing on file. Everything CallBaby stores is on your device and directly available to you there. For advertising data, please contact Google.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

9. Children

CallBaby is aimed at parents, that is, at adults. The app is not intended for use by children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes

When the app changes, this policy changes with it. The current version is always available at this address; the date at the top states when it was last revised.
