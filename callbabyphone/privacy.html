There is no CallBaby user account and no server operated by us. Google also processes data for advertising, privacy choices and the optional Pro purchase.

2. What the app does

CallBaby Babyphone turns a smartphone into a baby monitor. The device stays in the nursery and listens. When the sound crosses the configured threshold — by loudness or by detected crying, as you choose — the app attempts to call a phone number you have entered. Speakerphone on the child’s device is optional and off by default. A second device running the app is not required; the alert travels over the ordinary mobile network.

3. Processing on the device

3.1 Microphone

While monitoring, the app accesses the microphone. Audio is analysed continuously in memory and discarded immediately.

  • No recording is stored — neither in full nor in fragments, neither permanently nor temporarily on the device's storage.
  • No audio is uploaded for sound detection. Local analysis does not require a server.
  • Cry detection uses a classification model bundled inside the app package that runs entirely on the device. No audio leaves the device for it either.
  • During an alert or test call, room audio travels over the ordinary telephone connection. Your telephone provider processes connection data and call content under its own terms. The selected recipient can hear the audio; this may also be a voicemail service.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract). The microphone permission is the core purpose of the app; without it the app has no function. You can revoke it at any time in the Android settings.

3.2 Phone number and contact name

The phone number you enter, and any name stored alongside it, are stored in your device's shared preferences (SharedPreferences). The app does not send them to a server operated by us. To place a call, the number is passed to Android telephony and your telephone provider. If you pick a number through the system contact picker, Android hands the app only that one selected number; the app does not read your address book and holds no permission to do so.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Deletion: uninstall the app or clear its data in the Android settings.

3.3 Phone state

To coordinate dialing, the call and resuming detection afterwards, the app reads the device’s call state (READ_PHONE_STATE). This state does not reliably distinguish dialing from an answered call; the app cannot tell whether a person or voicemail answers. Numbers of incoming calls, call logs and your own number are neither read, stored nor transmitted.

3.4 Settings and diagnostics

Your settings — sensitivity, trigger type, start delay, language — live on the device. If the monitoring service fails, the app records the technical error message locally as well, so the cause can be traced on the device. No crash or usage statistics are transmitted to us. Diagnostic data processed by the advertising SDK is described in section 5. Depending on the Android backup settings, general app settings may be included in device backups; the separate purchase storage is excluded.

6. Optional Pro purchase

The following purchase information applies to app versions that offer CallBaby Pro.

CallBaby Pro is a one-time purchase that removes ads and unlocks silent mode. Alert and test calls remain unlimited without a purchase. Google Play handles the transaction; the app does not receive payment instruments such as credit card details. Google’s privacy policy governs payment processing.

The app receives a purchase receipt and signature from Google Play, verifies them locally and stores the verified entitlement separately from general settings. This purchase storage is excluded from Android backup. Nothing is sent to a server operated by us. Purchases are checked with Google Play on startup and when returning to the app; you can restore them using the Google Play account used to buy Pro. A previously verified purchase remains available locally during network failures. A successful check without a valid purchase removes the entitlement, for example after a refund.

Legal basis for processing purchase evidence: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing purchased features). Clearing app data or uninstalling removes the local evidence; it does not delete your purchase from Google Play.

7. Children

CallBaby Babyphone is aimed at parents, not at children. The app is not intended for use by children and is not marketed to children. Local sound detection also processes sounds made by the child; during a telephone call the selected recipient may hear the child’s voice.

8. Retention

Local settings and diagnostic messages remain until you change them, clear app data or uninstall the app. Purchase evidence is also removed after a successful check without a valid purchase. Google’s retention rules apply to advertising, privacy choices and purchases; your telephone provider’s rules apply to telephone connections.
